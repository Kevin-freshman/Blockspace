What this can—and cannot—say

Overlap and timing are exploratory co-occurrence signals. Public information arrival, shared strategy constraints, market popularity, bots reacting to the same price move, and data duplication are plausible alternatives.

They are not evidence of insider trading, wash trading, or collusion. No causal or identity linkage is inferred.
