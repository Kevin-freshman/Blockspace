Glossary and reading guide

Definitions used consistently across all pages.

Bounded window

The configured time or row-limited slice analyzed; never the full account history.

Observed notional

The sum of size × price in API quote units; not P&L or wealth.

HHI

Σ market notional share². Higher values mean more concentrated observed notional.

Saturation

A page/window hit its API page-size boundary and may require subdivision.

Burst

A descriptive short-interval heuristic over retained trades, not an intent label.

Reversal/churn

Opposite observed directions close in time under a fixed heuristic.

Unknown metadata

A conditionId without resolved market metadata; never treated as non-crypto.
