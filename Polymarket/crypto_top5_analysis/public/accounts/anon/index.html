Interpretation boundary

Burst and reversal/churn labels are deterministic heuristics, not intent classifiers. Shared markets and close timestamps can arise from public news, common market-making incentives, API timestamp granularity, popular markets, or correlated strategies.

These observations cannot establish insider trading, wash trading, or collusion. Capped trades, bounded closed positions, unresolved market metadata, and snapshot endpoint semantics constrain every inference.
